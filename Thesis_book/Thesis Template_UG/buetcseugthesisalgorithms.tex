\chapter{Algorithms}\label{ch:algorithms}

This appendix collects the principal algorithms that underpin VigiDroid's offline training pipeline, on-device cascade orchestration, and metrics post-processing. Each procedure is stated at the level of abstraction used in our implementation, omitting language-specific details while preserving the semantics required for PC--device parity.
